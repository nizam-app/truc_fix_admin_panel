FAQ

Fast answers for public support and app store review.

Can I get help without logging in?

Yes. This support page is public and does not require login, registration, or an active TruckFix account.

Is this page for both Android and iOS users?

Yes. Select iOS app or Android app in the support form so the team can route the request correctly.

What should I do in a roadside emergency?

Contact emergency services first if anyone is at risk or there is immediate danger. TruckFix support is for platform, booking, and account help.

How quickly will TruckFix reply?

TruckFix aims to respond within 24 hours. Complex billing, payout, safety, or dispute requests may take longer to review.
